## Scope

App shell for every DreamJob route: header, footer, theme toggle, home first viewport, guard states. Visitor mode: Operate (the home search is a task entry, not a pitch).

Audience: job seekers in Georgia, employers, admins. Task: get from search to a credible vacancy fast; employers reach their dashboard. Constraints: light and dark themes, phone to desktop, no fabricated counts or employers.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A vacancy is a journey on the DreamJob network: lines are categories, stations are statuses. Refuses the white-header, blue-button job board with a filter sidebar.

OWN-WORLD: Midnight enamel (#0B1230) shell header in both themes with a 4px scarlet rule; porcelain ground in light, enamel ground in dark; line inks scarlet, cobalt, amber, green only for category, state, and the primary action. Condensed transit caps for display and tracked labels, Barlow body, tabular figures. Station node (ring on a line) is the one mark: logo, status track, active nav.

STORY: The seeker sees that every employer is verified and every salary is shown, types a role, and boards the vacancy list.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Enamel header (logo node left, Vacancies and For companies nav, theme toggle, sign in, scarlet Post a vacancy). Left 7 columns: condensed three-line headline, one-line support, search bar (keyword, city, Search). Right 5 columns: enamel line panel drawing three status stations and the four line inks as the product's promise. Below: three principle rows on line stripes.

FORM: Midnight transit diagram, challenger (won both axes; also position 1 on the grounded list), seed key 211ce828.
